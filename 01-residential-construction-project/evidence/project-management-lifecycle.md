## How the Areas Connected

### Scope

Defined what the project was intended to deliver and established project boundaries.

### Scheduling

Translated project activities into a planned sequence and timeline.

### Resources

Considered the people and resources required to complete project activities.

### Risk

Identified potential factors that could affect project delivery and considered appropriate responses.

### Quality

Supported the accuracy, consistency, completeness, and presentation of project deliverables.

### Delivery

Brought the individual planning components together into an integrated project-management approach.

## My Perspective

As the Quality Assurance team member, my role provided visibility across multiple project deliverables.

This helped me understand that project quality depends on the consistency between interconnected project-management activities rather than on individual documents alone.
