# Risk Register Analysis

## Overview

Risk management was incorporated into the Residential Construction Project to identify and assess factors that could affect project objectives.

The project risk register evaluated risks using likelihood, consequence, control adequacy, risk level, and priority.

---

## Risk Assessment Framework

The project used a structured assessment approach:

```text
Risk Identification
        ↓
Likelihood Assessment
        ↓
Consequence Assessment
        ↓
Control Adequacy
        ↓
Risk Level
        ↓
Priority
        ↓
Treatment & Monitoring
```

This approach allowed the project team to prioritise risks according to their potential effect on project delivery.

---

## Identified Project Risks

The documented risk register included:

| Priority | Risk                               | Likelihood | Consequence | Risk Level |
| -------: | ---------------------------------- | ---------: | ----------: | ---------- |
|        1 | Council approval delays            |          4 |           4 | High       |
|        2 | Natural hazards — bushfires/floods |          3 |           5 | High       |
|        3 | Supply chain disruptions           |          3 |           3 | Medium     |
|        4 | Contractor performance issues      |          3 |           4 | High       |
|        5 | Budget overruns                    |          2 |           4 | Medium     |
|        6 | Wildlife damage to garden          |          2 |           3 | Medium     |

The risk register therefore considered risks across regulatory, environmental, procurement, contractor, financial, and site-related areas.

---

## Risk Treatment

The project developed treatment options for identified risks.

Examples included:

* Early submission of council applications
* Use of fire-resistant materials and flood-resilient design considerations
* Pre-ordering materials and considering alternative suppliers
* Contractor vetting and performance requirements

The treatment plan also assigned responsible roles, timelines, monitoring activities, and review dates.

---

## Integration with Project Planning

Risk mitigation was not treated as a separate document-only activity.

The project incorporated specific mitigation activities into the:

* Work Breakdown Structure
* Network Diagram
* Gantt Chart

Examples included:

```text
Council applications
        ↓
Bushfire-resistant materials
        ↓
Advanced material orders
        ↓
Contractor screening
```

This demonstrated how risk responses can become actual project activities rather than remaining only as statements within a risk register.

---

## Risk Monitoring

The risk plan established ongoing review mechanisms, including:

* Fortnightly reviews
* Continuous monitoring
* Phase-based assessments
* Stakeholder communication
* Change documentation
* Event-driven reassessment

This supported the principle that project risks should be monitored throughout the project lifecycle.

## Key Learning

The project demonstrated that risk management is most useful when it is connected to project planning.

A risk identified in the register can influence:

```text
Risk
 ↓
Response
 ↓
Project Activity
 ↓
Schedule
 ↓
Resources
 ↓
Project Delivery
```

This helped me understand risk management as an active project-control process rather than simply a documentation requirement.
