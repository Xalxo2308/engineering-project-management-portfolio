## Evidence Principles

The portfolio follows four principles:

### 1. Accuracy

Claims are based on documented project work and individual reflections.

### 2. Transparency

Team deliverables are not presented as solely my individual work unless supported by direct evidence.

### 3. Professional Relevance

Academic project activities are translated into transferable engineering and project-management capabilities.

### 4. Evidence Boundaries

The portfolio does not claim professional construction-site management, independent project-manager authority, or ownership of the entire project.
