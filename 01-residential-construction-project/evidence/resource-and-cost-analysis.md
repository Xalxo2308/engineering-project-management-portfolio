# Resource and Cost Management Analysis

## Overview

Resource and cost management formed part of the integrated project-management plan for the Residential Construction Project.

The project considered human resources, equipment, materials, labour requirements, and project costs in relation to the planned construction activities.

---

## Resource Management

The project allocated defined responsibilities across the project team.

The project-management team included roles such as:

| Role              | Project Responsibility       |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Team Leader       | Leadership and coordination  |
| Quality Assurance | QA checks and documentation  |
| Risk Manager      | Risk register and mitigation |
| Resource Planner  | Resource allocation          |
| Scheduler         | Task scheduling and updates  |

This structure provided defined ownership for different project-management activities.

---

## My Role

My assigned project role was **Quality Assurance**.

The resource-management documentation identifies my responsibilities as:

* QA checks
* Documentation review
* Quality assurance activities throughout the project

My role therefore supported the quality and reliability of project deliverables rather than ownership of resource allocation or cost planning.

---

## Resource Categories

The project considered several categories of resources.

### Human Resources

Personnel were allocated according to project responsibilities and construction requirements.

### Equipment

Project planning considered equipment required to support construction activities.

### Materials

Construction materials were considered in relation to the planned work and project phases.

### Project Team Resources

The project also required resources for planning, communication, documentation, scheduling, and project coordination.

---

## Resource–Schedule Relationship

Resource planning was connected to the project schedule.

```text
Project Activities
       ↓
Resource Requirements
       ↓
Resource Allocation
       ↓
Schedule Planning
       ↓
Project Delivery
```

A change in project activities or timing could therefore affect resource requirements.

---

## Cost Management

The project also incorporated cost planning to support delivery within the defined financial framework.

Cost considerations included:

* Materials
* Labour
* Approvals and permits
* Project activities
* Contingency considerations

The resource and cost plan used estimated costs to provide an overall view of the financial requirements of the project.

---

## Cost–Schedule Relationship

Time and cost were considered interconnected project constraints.

```text
Schedule Delay
      ↓
Additional Labour / Resource Requirements
      ↓
Potential Additional Cost
      ↓
Impact on Project Budget
```

This relationship demonstrates why resource, schedule, and risk management need to be considered together.

---

## Resource and Risk Relationship

Resource availability was also connected to project risk.

For example, delays in obtaining required materials or resources could affect planned construction activities.

This reinforced the importance of considering resource availability when developing project schedules and risk responses.

---

## Quality Perspective

From my Quality Assurance perspective, resource and cost documentation also required clear and consistent information.

QA review of project documentation helped support:

* Consistent presentation of project information
* Accurate tables
* Clear documentation
* Integration between project deliverables
* Reliable information for the final project report

My responsibility was therefore connected to the quality of the project's documentation rather than direct management of the project budget.

## Key Learning

The project helped me understand that resources and costs cannot be considered independently from the project schedule.

A change in scope, activity duration, resource availability, or risk response can influence other areas of project performance.

My Quality Assurance role also showed me that accurate documentation is important because project decisions depend on reliable project information.
