# Quality Assurance

## Overview

Quality Assurance was my assigned responsibility within the Residential Construction Project team.

My role focused on maintaining consistency, accuracy, completeness, and presentation quality across the project's documentation.

Rather than treating QA as a final-stage activity, I applied review and verification throughout the development of the project deliverables.

---

## QA Responsibilities

My documented Quality Assurance responsibilities included:

* Reviewing project documentation
* Checking formatting and document consistency
* Verifying tables and project information
* Checking referencing and APA 7 formatting
* Reviewing spelling and grammar
* Supporting document version control
* Reviewing schedule information
* Checking the integration of team members' contributions
* Conducting final document reviews before submission

---

The project documentation established a structured checklist for reviewing deliverables.

| QA Area            | Review Activity                                  |

## Quality Control vs Quality Assurance

An important distinction I learned through the project was the difference between quality assurance and simply correcting errors.

**Quality control** focuses on identifying problems in completed outputs.

**Quality assurance** is broader: it establishes a process for reducing errors and maintaining quality throughout project delivery.

In this project, my QA contribution therefore involved both reviewing individual outputs and considering how different project documents fitted together.

---

## Document Integration

The project consisted of multiple interconnected deliverables.

For example:

```text
Project Scope
      ↓
Project Activities
      ↓
Schedule / Gantt Chart
      ↓
Resources & Costs
      ↓
Risk Management
      ↓
Final Project Report
```

Changes or inconsistencies in one area could affect other project documents.

My QA role therefore required attention not only to individual documents but also to the consistency of information across the project.

---

## Version Control

Collaborative document development required the team to manage multiple revisions.

OneDrive was used to support document sharing and version control.

From a QA perspective, version control was important because it helped the team work from current documents and reduced the risk of incorporating outdated information into the final submission.

---

## QA and Scheduling

Scheduling was another area reviewed as part of my QA responsibilities.

I reviewed the Gantt Chart and checked project activity durations for accuracy.

This demonstrates an important project-management principle: schedule information needs to be reviewed for consistency and accuracy because it affects the overall representation of project progress and delivery.

---

## Key Learning

My main learning from the QA role was that quality is an integrated project-management responsibility.

A project can contain technically correct individual documents while still having problems if those documents are inconsistent with one another.

The experience reinforced the importance of:

* Structured review processes
* Attention to detail
* Document control
* Consistent project information
* Clear communication between team members
* Reviewing deliverables before final submission
