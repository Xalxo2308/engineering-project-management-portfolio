# Project Scheduling

## Overview

Scheduling was a key component of the Residential Construction Project management plan.

The schedule translated the project's scope into sequenced activities and provided a basis for understanding project duration, dependencies, and planned delivery.

The project used a **Gantt Chart** as part of its scheduling documentation.

---

## Scheduling Approach

The project schedule was developed by considering:

* Project activities
* Activity sequencing
* Dependencies
* Activity durations
* Project phases
* Planned project completion
* Resource requirements
* Relationships between project deliverables

The schedule was connected to the project's broader Work Breakdown Structure and project planning documentation.

---

## Project Phases

The residential construction project was structured around major phases including:

```text
Site Preparation
        ↓
Construction
        ↓
External Works
        ↓
Final Inspection & Handover
```

Activities within these phases were organised to establish a logical sequence for project delivery.

---

## Gantt Chart

A Gantt Chart was used to represent the planned schedule visually.

It provided a way to communicate:

* When activities were planned to begin
* Activity durations
* The sequence of activities
* Relationships between activities
* Overall project timing

The project documentation records the use of **Microsoft Project** for project scheduling and related planning outputs.

---

## My Role in Schedule Quality Assurance

My assigned project role was **Quality Assurance**, rather than project scheduling.

As part of my QA responsibilities, I reviewed the Gantt Chart and checked the accuracy of project activity durations.

This involved reviewing the schedule as a project deliverable and identifying potential inconsistencies before the final submission.

The project review log records the Gantt Chart review as one of my documented QA activities.

---

## Schedule Quality Checks

The schedule review considered:

| Check             | Purpose                                                                                        |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Activity Duration | Confirm that planned durations were accurate                                                   |
| Sequencing        | Ensure activities followed a logical project sequence                                          |
| Consistency       | Check schedule information against other project documentation                                 |
| Presentation      | Ensure the Gantt Chart was clear and readable                                                  |
| Integration       | Confirm that scheduling information could be incorporated into the final project documentation |

---

## Schedule and Project Integration

The schedule was not developed in isolation.

It was connected to other project-management components:

```text
Project Scope
      ↓
Project Activities
      ↓
Activity Dependencies
      ↓
Project Schedule
      ↓
Resource Requirements
      ↓
Risk Considerations
      ↓
Project Delivery
```

This integration is important because changes to activities or dependencies can affect project duration and resource requirements.

---

## Scheduling Lessons

The project demonstrated the importance of maintaining accurate scheduling information throughout project planning.

A schedule is more than a visual timeline. It provides a structured representation of how project activities fit together and helps the project team understand the timing and dependencies associated with delivery.

From my QA perspective, I learned that reviewing schedule information is important because inaccurate durations or inconsistent project information can affect the credibility of the overall project plan.
