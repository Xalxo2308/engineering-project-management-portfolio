# My Contribution

## Role: Quality Assurance

For the Residential Construction Project, I was assigned the role of **Quality Assurance** within the project team.

My contribution focused on maintaining the quality, consistency, accuracy, and integration of the team's project documentation throughout the project.

---

## Responsibilities

My documented responsibilities included:

### 1. Documentation Quality

I reviewed project documentation to ensure that deliverables were clearly structured, consistently formatted, and suitable for integration into the final project submission.

### 2. Accuracy Checks

I reviewed tables and project information against the available source material to identify inconsistencies or errors.

### 3. Referencing

I checked project documentation for consistency with **APA 7 referencing requirements**.

### 4. Spelling and Grammar

I conducted spelling and grammar checks using Grammarly together with manual review.

### 5. Version Control

I supported document version control through **OneDrive**, helping the team maintain updated versions of project documents during collaborative development.

### 6. Schedule Review

As part of the QA process, I reviewed the Gantt Chart and checked the accuracy of project activity durations.

### 7. Final Document Review

I reviewed project deliverables before final submission and helped ensure that individual contributions were integrated into the overall project documentation.

## QA Checklist

The project resource and cost management documentation recorded the following QA checks:

* **Formatting:** Consistent style and layout
* **Referencing:** APA 7 format checked
* **Table Accuracy:** Values reviewed against source files
* **Spelling and Grammar:** Grammarly and manual review
* **Version Control:** Documents tracked through OneDrive

---

## My Approach to Quality Assurance

My approach was to treat quality assurance as an ongoing project activity rather than a final proofreading task.

The review process involved checking individual documents while also considering how the documents connected with one another. This was important because project-management deliverables such as scope, scheduling, risk, resources, and the final report need to remain consistent throughout the project.

---

## What I Learned

The Quality Assurance role showed me that effective project delivery depends not only on completing individual tasks but also on ensuring that the outputs produced by different team members work together as a coherent project.

I also learned the importance of structured document review, version control, accuracy checks, and early identification of inconsistencies.
