# Project Governance

## Overview

Project governance established how the Residential Construction Project team organised responsibilities, communication, documentation, and project controls.

The project used defined team roles and collaborative processes to support accountability and coordination throughout project development.

---

## Team Structure

The project team divided responsibilities across different project-management areas.

The team charter assigned specific responsibilities to team members, including areas such as:

* Quality Assurance
* Scheduling
* Risk Management
* Scope and Work Breakdown Structure
* Resource and Cost Management

This division of responsibility provided clearer ownership of project-management activities while requiring coordination between team members.

---

## My Governance Role

My assigned role was **Quality Assurance**.

My responsibility was to support the quality and consistency of project deliverables and help ensure that outputs from different team members could be integrated into the final project documentation.

My documented QA activities included:

* Reviewing project documents
* Checking formatting and consistency
* Verifying tables and information
* Checking references
* Reviewing schedule information
* Supporting version control
* Conducting final document reviews

---

## Team Communication

The team used collaborative communication channels to coordinate project work.

The documented team processes included the use of:

* Microsoft Teams
* WhatsApp
* Shared project documents

These channels supported communication, file sharing, coordination, and project updates.

---

## Meetings

Team meetings were used to coordinate project activities and discuss project progress.

Meeting records documented participation and project discussions, while meeting minutes provided a record of decisions and actions.

Maintaining meeting records supported transparency and accountability within the project team.

---

## Document Control

Document control was an important component of the project's governance approach.

The team used shared document storage and version control to manage project files during collaborative development.

From my QA perspective, document control helped reduce the risk of reviewing or incorporating outdated versions of project documents.

---

## Roles and Accountability

Defining individual responsibilities helped establish accountability for different areas of the project.

The governance structure can be represented as:

```text id="6j3qhv"
Team Charter
     │
     ├── Defined Roles
     │
     ├── Assigned Responsibilities
     │
     ├── Communication Processes
     │
     ├── Meeting Records
     │
     └── Document / Version Control
              │
              ↓
       Integrated Project Output
```

This structure provided a framework for coordinating individual contributions into a single project-management plan.

---

## Quality and Governance

Quality assurance was connected to project governance because reliable project documentation depends on clear responsibilities and controlled information.

My QA activities supported governance by:

* Reviewing outputs against expected standards
* Identifying inconsistencies
* Supporting document control
* Checking project information
* Helping integrate team contributions

This demonstrated how quality management can support broader project governance rather than operating as a separate activity.

---

## Decision and Issue Management

The project team used meetings and collaborative communication to discuss project matters and coordinate work.

Where project documents required changes or clarification, team members could communicate and update the relevant deliverables.

The use of documented responsibilities and shared project files provided a basic framework for coordinating these changes.

---

## Lessons from Project Governance

The project demonstrated the importance of clearly defined responsibilities in team-based project delivery.

I learned that effective governance does not necessarily require complex structures. Even within a student project environment, clear roles, communication processes, meeting records, and document control can improve accountability and coordination.

---
