## About This Portfolio

This repository presents a project-management case study developed during my postgraduate engineering studies at the **University of Technology Sydney (UTS)**.

The portfolio focuses on one project:

### Residential Construction Project

A project-management case study involving the planning and delivery of a four-bedroom retirement home in Forster, New South Wales.

The project was completed as part of **UTS 49002 – Managing Projects**.
